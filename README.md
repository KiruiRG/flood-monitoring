# flood-monitoring

In Kenya, flooding is a frequent problem, especially in low-lying areas, river basins, and urban informal settlements. These incidents often cause property damage, community dislodgment, fatalities, and disruptions to vital infrastructure. Effective flood monitoring and response are still challenging, even with the growing availability of environmental data. 
The prompt distribution of flood warnings and coordination between responding agencies remain a significant challenge. Current solutions are frequently divided, concentrating on specific tasks like forecasting or alarm creation without offering a single platform for decision-making and communication. As a result, certain vulnerable areas are more at risk of flooding because they do not receive timely warnings. 
This project proposes the establishment of a flood monitoring and government coordination platform for Kenya. To forecast flood risks, the system will combine environmental data from several sources and use a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) model. It will offer a centralized dashboard for government collaboration and a mobile application for alerts and incident reporting.
The proposed solution was chosen because it integrates coordination, communication, monitoring, and prediction on a single platform. It is anticipated that the system will strengthen emergency response capabilities, facilitate well-informed decision-making, and increase flood preparedness, ultimately safeguarding people's lives and property in Kenya.
